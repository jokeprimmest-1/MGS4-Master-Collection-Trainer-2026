## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **METAL GEAR SOLID 4: Guns of the Patriots - Master Collection Version**. This trainer provides optional single-player enhancements for Old Snake's final mission, including unlimited health, ammunition, items, Drebin Points, battery power, and suppressor durability. It can also prevent alerts, stabilize Psyche, and lock OctoCamo settings.

**Target Audience:** Players who want accessibility options, relaxed exploration, experimentation with weapons and equipment, or a more convenient replay of the single-player campaign.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Infinite Health** — Prevents Old Snake's life gauge from decreasing during gameplay.
- **Maximum Psyche** — Keeps Snake's Psyche at maximum to reduce the effects of stress and combat pressure.
- **No Alert** — Prevents enemies from placing the area into an alert state after detection.
- **Infinite Drebin Points** — Provides unlimited Drebin Points for purchasing weapons, ammunition, upgrades, and equipment.
- **Infinite Items** — Prevents consumable equipment such as rations, syringes, grenades, and chaff grenades from being depleted.
- **Infinite Battery** — Keeps electronic equipment such as the Solid Eye and night-vision functions powered.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **Infinite Ammo** — Prevents ammunition reserves from being consumed by equipped firearms.
- **No Reload** — Keeps the current magazine supplied so compatible weapons can fire without manual reloading.
- **Infinite Suppressor Durability** — Stops equipped weapon suppressors from wearing out during extended use.

### 📌 Stealth

- **Lock OctoCamo** — Locks Snake's current OctoCamo appearance instead of allowing it to change with surfaces and surroundings.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Snake can move while crouching, crawl while prone, take cover, roll, climb, and perform context-sensitive actions. Use No Alert when practicing routes through guarded areas, and use Lock OctoCamo to maintain a preferred camouflage appearance while moving between environments.

### Tools & Inventory

Weapons, ammunition, equipment, and consumables are managed through the in-game inventory. Infinite Ammo and Infinite Items reduce resource management, while Infinite Drebin Points allows purchases and weapon customization through Drebin's shop. Disable inventory-related options before buying, selling, or rearranging equipment if an item display does not update immediately.

### World & Environment

The campaign moves through war zones and other heavily guarded locations where patrols, cameras, environmental cover, and battlefield conditions influence stealth. No Alert supports uninterrupted exploration, while Infinite Battery keeps the Solid Eye and related electronic functions available for navigation and observation.

### Combat & Weapons

MGS4 combines third-person shooting, close-quarters combat, stealth takedowns, cover mechanics, weapon customization, and encounters with hostile soldiers, machines, and bosses. Infinite Health and Maximum Psyche improve survivability, while Infinite Ammo, No Reload, and Infinite Suppressor Durability support extended firefights and weapon testing.
